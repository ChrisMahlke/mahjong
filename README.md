# Mahjong Four Ways: Support

Support and privacy policy for **Mahjong Four Ways**, an iPhone game by Chris Mahlke that brings four of the world's great mahjong games to one table: Hong Kong, Japanese Riichi, Chinese Official (MCR) and American.

## Get help

- **Found a bug or have a question?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/mahjong-support/issues/new) on this repository. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the ruleset you were playing and what happened.
- **Have an idea?** Issues are welcome for suggestions too.

The app needs no account or sign-in, so there is nothing to reset or recover. Your progress, saved games and settings live on your device and in its own backups.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the app free?**
Yes. There are no in-app purchases, no ads and no currency. Every tile set, mat, charm, frame and tournament is included.

**Can I play offline?**
Yes. Every ruleset can be played solo against the computer with no internet. Nearby play connects phones directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, also without internet. Online play with friends uses Apple's Game Center.

**Which rulesets are included?**
Hong Kong (fan scoring and flowers), Japanese Riichi (World Riichi Championship 2025 rules), Chinese Official (MCR, all 81 fans) and American (the Charleston, jokers and an original card of 45 hands). House rules for Hong Kong, Riichi and American can be saved as presets.

**Which languages are supported?**
English, Traditional Chinese (with Hong Kong terms), Simplified Chinese and Japanese. You can switch at any time in Settings or from the table menu, even mid-game.

**Does it support accessibility features?**
Yes: VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion.

**I'm new to mahjong. Where do I start?**
Open Learn for 27 short, hands-on lessons. At the table, the coach suggests a discard and explains why, and every winning hand is scored line by line.

## Privacy Policy for Mahjong Four Ways

*Last updated: October 2026*

Mahjong Four Ways does not collect any personal data. The developer does not receive, store or share any information about you.

### What stays on your device

Your progress (XP, levels, achievements, tournament results), saved games, settings, lesson progress and hand reviews are stored only on your device and in your device's own backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

### Playing with other people

When you play with friends, the app sends what a game needs directly to the other players at your table: your player name (your Game Center nickname if you are signed in, otherwise the name you chose, otherwise "Player"), an identifier for the game, your moves, and the stickers and set phrases you choose to send. Online games use Apple's Game Center; nearby games connect devices directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. A device hosting a nearby table shows its player's name to people nearby who are looking for a game. Nothing typed is ever sent.

### Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your nickname and photo are read from Game Center to show in the app. Game Center is provided by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### No tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it does not use your data to track you across apps or websites.

### Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
