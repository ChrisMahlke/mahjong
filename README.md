Mahjong Four Ways is a free iPhone game with four rulesets: Hong Kong, Japanese Riichi, Chinese Official (MCR), and American. No ads, no purchases, no sign-in.

This repository is the support channel for the app. To report a problem or ask a question, [open an issue](https://github.com/ChrisMahlke/mahjong/issues/new) with your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

## Privacy policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

Mahjong Four Ways collects no personal data, and the developer receives nothing about you. Progress, saved games, and settings stay on your device and in its backups. When you play with others, the app sends only what a game needs directly to the other players: your player name, a game identifier, your moves, and the stickers or set phrases you choose, through Apple's Game Center or a nearby Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connection. If you're signed in, your Game Center nickname and photo are read under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). There is no advertising, analytics, or tracking, and the app is suitable for all ages. Changes to this policy will be posted here.
